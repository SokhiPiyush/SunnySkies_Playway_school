Our Vision and Mission

Our Vision

Our Vision is to enhance your child's potential for success in their holistic developments.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a progressive immersion program that addresses each child's holistic development (physical, social, emotional and cognitive) and fosters individuality, confidence and creativity in a stimulating, fun and safe preschool.

Teaching Methods

We provide high quality care and education for children below statutory school age; work in partnership with parents to help children to learn and develop; add to the life and well-being of the local community; and offer children and their parents a service that promotes equality and values diversity.

Curriculum

Social and emotional development to foster a child's sense of self, enhance his or her ability to make friends, and create openness to learning. Physical development to strengthen large and small muscles, coordinate movement, and improve overall health and growth. Creative expression and aesthetic development to foster a child's appreciation for the arts music, movement, dramatic play, and visual arts.

Objective

Express feelings and develop emotional intelligence through guided activities and supportive learning environments.

Admissions Programme

At SUNNY Skies Play School, we conduct the following programme, keeping in view the active and experimental learning requirements of the child:

PROGRAMME AGE
Toddler's Club 1.2 – 1.8 years
Play Group 1.8 – 3 years
Nursery 3 – 4 years
Jr KG 4 – 5 years
Sr KG 5 – 6 years
Day care 1.5 – 12 years
Activity centre 2 – 12 years

Admission in SUNNY Skies play School is based on a rolling enrollment system. Parents may enroll children at any time. Acceptance is based on space availability. A registration fee is required and can be applied to the wait list if space is not currently available.

We provide a safe, joyful and creative learning environment for kids. Our mission is to build confidence, creativity and curiosity in every child.
